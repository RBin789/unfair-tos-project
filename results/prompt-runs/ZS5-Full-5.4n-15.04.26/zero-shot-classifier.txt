## LEGAL CATEGORIES & CRITERIA
### **0. Limitation of Liability**
    Unfair: Excludes or restricts provider liability for non-performance, personal injury, or gross negligence.
    Fair: Clauses that are transparent, proportional, and explicitly protect the consumer's mandatory statutory rights."

### **1. Unilateral Tvermination**
    Definition: Terms allowing the provider to dissolve the contract at will.
    Unfair: Allowing the provider to dissolve the contract "at will" or without reasonable notice or reciprocal rights for the consumer.
    Fair: Specifying objective grounds for termination and providing a sufficient notice period for the consumer to react."

### **2. Unilateral Change**
    Unfair: Altering terms or prices without a valid, pre-specified reason or the consumer's consent.
    Fair: Providing advance notice, valid justification, and the right for the consumer to terminate the contract immediately without penalty."

### **3. Content Removal**
    Unfair: Granting the provider arbitrary rights to delete or hide user content without a clear reason or notice.
    Fair: Specifying objective grounds for removal and offering a transparent notification and appeal process."

### **4. Contract by Using**
    Unfair: "Browse-wrap" agreements where mere use is deemed acceptance, binding consumers without informed consent.
    Fair: "Click-wrap" agreements where acceptance is an affirmative action taken after terms are clearly presented."

### **5. Choice of Law**
    Unfair: Selecting a foreign legal system specifically to deprive consumers of their home country’s mandatory protections.
    Fair: Explicitly preserving the consumer’s right to rely on their local mandatory legal safeguards."

### **6. Jurisdiction**
    Unfair: Forcing consumers to litigate in a distant or inconvenient forum, effectively hindering their access to justice.
    Fair: Allowing the consumer to bring legal proceedings in the courts of their own domicile."

### **7. Arbitration**
    Unfair: Imposing mandatory, binding arbitration that prevents the consumer from seeking remedies in a court of law.
    Fair: Offering arbitration as an optional, low-cost, and non-binding alternative."

### **8-Fair:** This category is a catch-all for any clause that is balanced, transparent, and does not meet the criteria for any of the unfair categories (0–7). Use this label only if no unfair elements are present.
